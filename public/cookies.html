Cookie Policy

How we use cookies and similar technologies on our website

Effective Date: December 2, 2025

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit our website. They help us recognize you, remember your preferences, and understand how you use our site. Cookies are essential for delivering a personalized and secure online experience.

How Cookies Work

When you visit Luxe Jewelry:

  • Our server places a cookie on your device
  • The cookie contains a unique identifier and preference data
  • When you visit again, your browser sends the cookie back to us
  • We read the cookie to remember your preferences and activity

Other Similar Technologies

We also use similar tracking technologies:

  • Web Beacons: Tiny graphics used to track page views and email interactions
  • Pixels: Invisible pixels that track conversions and user behavior
  • Local Storage: Browser-based storage for persistent data

Types of Cookies We Use

1. Essential Cookies REQUIRED

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable:

  • User authentication and login sessions
  • Shopping cart functionality
  • Checkout process and payment
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Website stability and performance

Your choice: These cookies cannot be disabled as they're essential for website functionality.

2. Performance & Analytics Cookies OPTIONAL

These cookies help us understand how you use our website:

  • Google Analytics - tracks page views, user behavior, traffic sources
  • Performance monitoring - measures page load times and errors
  • Session recording (optional) - records anonymous user sessions
  • A/B testing - tests different website versions

Your choice: You can opt out of these cookies. They don't affect website functionality.

3. Marketing & Advertising Cookies OPTIONAL

These cookies are used to deliver personalized advertising:

  • Facebook Pixel - for Facebook retargeting ads
  • Google Ads conversion tracking - tracks purchase conversions
  • Advertising network tracking - across multiple websites
  • Email marketing pixels - tracks email opens and clicks

Your choice: You can disable these to prevent personalized advertising.

4. Preference & Functionality Cookies OPTIONAL

These cookies remember your choices and preferences:

  • Language preferences
  • Dark mode settings
  • Product search history
  • Previously viewed items
  • Currency selection

Your choice: Disabling these will not affect functionality but may reduce personalization.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Browser Settings

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Clear browsing data

Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner. You can:

  • Accept All: Accept all cookies
  • Accept Essential Only: Accept only essential cookies
  • Customize: Choose which cookie categories to accept

Changing Your Mind

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by:

  1. Clicking the cookie settings icon (if visible)
  2. Clearing your browser cookies and revisiting our site
  3. Contacting us to reset your preferences

Note: Disabling essential cookies may prevent the website from functioning properly, including preventing access to your account and completing purchases.

Privacy & Security

How We Protect Your Data

Cookies containing personal information are protected with:

  • SSL/TLS encryption during transmission
  • Secure flags to prevent access from scripts
  • HttpOnly flags to prevent unauthorized access
  • Short expiration times where possible

Do Not Track

If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal, we respect this preference for non-essential tracking. However, some cookies required for website functionality may still be used.

International Users

If you are accessing this website from the European Union or other jurisdictions with privacy regulations, your data may be subject to those protections.
