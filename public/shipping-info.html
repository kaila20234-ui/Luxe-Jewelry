How Our Shipping Process Works

Step 1: Order Confirmation

Once your order is placed and payment is confirmed, you'll receive an email confirmation with your order number and estimated delivery date within 1 hour.

Step 2: Processing

Our team carefully inspects and packages your jewelry in our signature Luxe Jewelry box with protective wrapping. This typically takes 1-2 business days.

Step 3: Dispatch

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number and link. You can monitor your package in real-time through our courier partner's tracking system.

Step 4: Delivery

Your package arrives at your doorstep. Signature may be required for high-value items. If you're not home, the courier will leave a notice with instructions for redelivery.

Step 5: Confirmation

Once delivered, you'll receive a delivery confirmation. Please inspect your package immediately and contact us if there are any issues.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you ship internationally?

Currently, we ship within Nigeria only. International shipping is coming soon. Contact us if you need clarification about your location.

Can I track my order?

Yes! Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number to monitor your package's location in real-time.

What if my package doesn't arrive on time?

Our couriers have a 99.8% on-time delivery rate. If there's a delay, we'll investigate immediately and keep you updated. Contact support for assistance.

Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

If your order hasn't shipped yet, contact us within 2 hours of purchase to change the address. Once shipped, contact the courier directly.

Is your packaging recyclable?

Yes! All our packaging is fully recyclable. We use eco-friendly materials to protect your jewelry while minimizing environmental impact.

What are the shipping charges?

Express: ₦2,500, Standard: ₦1,200, Economy: FREE on orders over ₦50,000. See above for details on each option.

Is my order insured during shipping?

Express and Standard shipments are fully insured. Economy shipments receive basic protection. Contact us for insurance on specific high-value items.
