Our Return Policy

We stand behind every piece of jewelry. If you're not completely satisfied, we make returns simple and hassle-free.

30-Day Return Window

You have 30 days from the date of delivery to return your item for a full refund or exchange. Items must be in original condition with all packaging and certificates included.

Full Refund Guarantee

Return any unused item within 30 days in original condition and receive a full refund of the purchase price. Refunds are processed within 7-10 business days to your original payment method.

Non-Returnable Items

Custom-made jewelry, engraved items, and clearance/sale items are non-returnable. Items damaged due to misuse cannot be returned. Please inspect items before completing purchase.

Free Return Shipping: For items that are defective or don't match the product description. Return shipping is customer-paid for change-of-mind returns.

How to Return an Item

Our return process is quick and straightforward. Follow these 5 simple steps:

1

Contact Our Support Team

Email support@luxejewelry.com or call +2349012892640 with your order number and reason for return. Include photos if applicable. We'll respond within 24 hours.

2

Receive Return Authorization

We'll send you a return authorization number (RMA) and prepaid shipping label (if applicable) via email within 24 hours. Keep the RMA number for your records.

3

Ship Your Item Back

Carefully pack your jewelry in the original Luxe Jewelry box with all documentation, certificates, and packaging. Use the provided shipping label and include your RMA number on the package.

4

We Inspect & Process

Once we receive your return, we inspect the item to verify it meets our return conditions. This typically takes 3-5 business days. We'll email you updates.

5

Receive Your Refund or Exchange

If approved, we'll process your refund or send your replacement item immediately. Refunds appear in your account within 7-10 business days.

Want to Exchange Instead?

Exchanges are simple and often faster than returns. Exchange your item for a different style, color, or size at no extra cost within 30 days.

Exchange Requirements

  • Original Luxe Jewelry box and packaging
  • All certificates and documentation included
  • Item must be in original condition
  • Exchange requested within 30 days of delivery
  • Size and color changes are free
  • If exchanging for higher-value item, pay the difference

What to Include in Your Return

To ensure your return is processed quickly, include the following:

  • The original jewelry item
  • Original Luxe Jewelry box and all packaging materials
  • All certificates of authenticity
  • Return authorization number (RMA) on the outside of package
  • Original receipt or order confirmation (if available)
  • A note with your order number and reason for return

Important: Items returned without proper packaging or documentation may be rejected. Always include your RMA number on the outside of the package in clear, visible writing.
