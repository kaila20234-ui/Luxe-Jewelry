Orders & Shopping

How do I place an order?

Placing an order is easy! Browse our collection, select your item, add it to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can pay using your preferred payment method. You'll receive an order confirmation email immediately.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept multiple payment methods including:

  • Paystack (Credit Card, Debit Card)
  • Bank Transfers
  • Mobile Money
  • PayPal
Can I cancel or modify my order?

Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours of placement. After that, your order enters our fulfillment process. For modifications, please contact our customer service team immediately at support@luxejewelry.com. We'll do our best to help!

Do you offer bulk or wholesale orders?

Yes! We offer special pricing for bulk and wholesale orders. Please contact our sales team at sales@luxejewelry.com with your requirements, and we'll provide you with a custom quote.

Shipping & Delivery

How long does shipping take?

Shipping times depend on your location and chosen delivery method:

  • Express Delivery (Kano): 1-2 business days
  • Standard Delivery (Nationwide): 3-5 business days
  • Economy Delivery: 7-10 business days
How much does shipping cost?

Shipping costs vary by location and delivery method. We offer free standard shipping on orders over ₦50,000! Check our Shipping Information page for detailed pricing.

Can I track my order?

Yes! Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can track your package in real-time through our courier partner's website.

What if my item arrives damaged?

If your item arrives damaged or defective, contact us within 48 hours with photos. We'll send a replacement immediately at no cost to you. We stand behind the quality of all our products!

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy! If you're not satisfied with your purchase, return it in original condition within 30 days for a full refund or exchange. Custom-made items are non-returnable.

How do I return an item?

Contact our customer service team with your order number. We'll send you a return authorization number and shipping label. Pack your item carefully and send it back to us. We'll process your refund within 7-10 business days.

Do you offer exchanges?

Absolutely! You can exchange your item for a different size, style, or color within 30 days. If the new item costs less, we'll refund the difference. If it costs more, you'll pay the difference.

How long does a refund take?

Once we receive and verify your return, we process your refund within 7-10 business days. The refund will be credited to your original payment method. Please allow up to 5 business days for the amount to appear in your account.

Products & Care

Are your jewels authentic?

Yes! All our jewelry is 100% authentic. We work directly with certified manufacturers and provide authenticity certificates with every purchase. Each item is carefully inspected for quality before shipping.

How do I care for my jewelry?

To keep your jewelry beautiful:

  • Remove jewelry during exercise, swimming, or bathing
  • Store in a cool, dry place
  • Clean gently with a soft cloth
  • Avoid contact with chemicals and perfumes
  • Get professional cleaning annually
Do you offer custom jewelry?

We offer custom designs! Contact our design team at design@luxejewelry.com with your ideas. We'll work with you to create a unique piece that matches your vision.

What is your warranty?

All items come with a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Damage from misuse, accidents, or normal wear and tear is not covered. For warranty claims, contact our service team.

Account & Login

How do I create an account?

Click the "Login" link at the top of the page, then select "Sign Up". Fill in your details and password. You'll receive a confirmation email to verify your account.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the login page, click "Forgot Password?" and enter your email address. We'll send you instructions to reset your password. Click the link in the email and create a new password.

How do I view my orders?

Log into your account and click on "My Orders". You'll see all your past and current orders with tracking information and status updates.
